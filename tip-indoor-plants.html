Easy Indoor Plants for Homes and Offices

Low-maintenance plants that grow well indoors, and how to look after them.

1Pick plants for your light

Money plant, snake plant, ZZ plant and peace lily cope well with indirect light. Keep flowering plants near bright windows.

2Don't overwater

Most indoor plants die from too much water, not too little. Water only when the top inch of soil feels dry.

3Use pots with drainage

Always use pots with a drainage hole and empty the saucer after watering so the roots don't rot.

4Wipe the leaves

Dust blocks light. Wipe leaves with a damp cloth every couple of weeks.

5Rotate your plants

Turn pots a quarter turn every week so the plant grows evenly instead of leaning towards the window.
