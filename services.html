Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Tell us about your space through the form, a phone call or WhatsApp, and we will discuss your requirements and give you a quotation at no cost.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Pallavaram and take up projects across Chennai. Contact us with your location and we will confirm.

Do you take up both homes and commercial properties?

Yes. We work on residential gardens and terraces as well as large commercial, corporate and institutional campuses.

Do you offer regular garden maintenance?

Yes. Along with new projects, we provide ongoing landscape maintenance, including lawn care, pruning, planting and irrigation upkeep.

Can you build a garden on my apartment terrace?

Yes. Terrace landscaping, vertical gardens and organic vegetable gardens are some of our core services.

Do you supply indoor plants for offices?

Yes. We supply indoor plants and also offer them on rental for offices and events.

How do I get a quotation?

Use the free estimate calculator for a rough idea, fill in the form on this page or the Contact page, or call us on 6379412122. Sharing a few photos of your space on WhatsApp helps us quote faster.
