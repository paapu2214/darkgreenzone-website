Landscape Project Design and Construction

From an empty plot to a finished garden, we plan and build complete landscapes for homes, apartments, offices and campuses. We study your space, sunlight, soil and budget, then design lawns, planting, walkways and features that suit how you use the space.

What’s included

  • Site visit and requirement study
  • Landscape layout and planting plan
  • Soil preparation and levelling
  • Lawn, shrub, tree and flower bed planting
  • Walkways, stepping stones, edging and pergolas
  • Garden lighting and irrigation integration
