About

Dark Green Zone

Since 2016, Dark Green Zone has grown from a one-man, one-truck licensed contracting business to a multidimensional landscaping firm offering clients landscape build/design and management services.

At the core of our business is a strong client relationship. Our staff is passionate and dedicated to high quality and efficiency. Our goal is not only to maintain your landscape to the highest standards, but also to enhance your property’s value. Our staff takes pride in suggesting and implementing programs and methods to achieve these goals to our client’s satisfaction.
