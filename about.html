About us

DARK GREEN ZONE

Since its establishment in 2016, Dark Green Zone has experienced significant growth and expansion. Starting as a one-man, one-truck licensed contracting business, it has now evolved into a full-fledged and multidimensional landscaping firm that provides comprehensive landscape build/design and management services to its clients.

At the heart of Dark Green Zone’s success lies a strong commitment to building and maintaining strong relationships with its clients. The company’s staff is made up of individuals who are passionate and dedicated to providing high-quality and efficient services. Their primary objective is to not only maintain their clients’ landscapes to the highest standards, but also to enhance the value of their properties. The staff takes pride in suggesting and implementing programs and methods that meet these goals and result in complete customer satisfaction.

Dark Green Zone’s expertise lies in the field of landscaping, which encompasses a wide range of activities, including planting and caring for trees, shrubs, flowers, ground cover, and grass. Additionally, the company also provides materials for these purposes. The company’s services also encompass various design elements, such as the construction of walkways, decks, retaining walls, installation of irrigation systems, creation of patios, and implementation of lighting solutions, among others. The aim of these design elements is to enhance the exterior of the building, creating a beautiful and functional outdoor space.
